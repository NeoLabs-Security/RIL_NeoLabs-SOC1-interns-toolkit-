## Research standard

Primary and authoritative sources take priority: official Wazuh and OpenSearch documentation, NIST publications, CISA guidance, MITRE ATT&CK, OWASP, Microsoft, AWS, Sigma and Docker documentation. Secondary sources may clarify practical behaviour but cannot override primary documentation.

Every substantial manual will include:

- learning objectives;
- terminology with plain-language explanations;
- worked examples;
- diagrams and screenshots where useful;
- analyst cautions and visibility limitations;
- guided exercises and review questions;
- references and version information.

## Publication rule

No material is treated as final until technical review, branding review, safety review and link/reference validation are complete.
