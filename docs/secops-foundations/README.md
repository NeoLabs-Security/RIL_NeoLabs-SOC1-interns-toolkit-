## How to study

For each module:

1. Read the professional definition.
2. Restate the plain-language explanation in your own words.
3. Work through the example without skipping the raw evidence.
4. Identify what the evidence proves, suggests and cannot prove.
5. Complete the review questions or guided investigation.
6. Record unfamiliar terms in your analyst glossary.

## VCC lab boundary

Examples in these notes use synthetic records, reserved example networks and authorised VCC scenarios. They do not grant permission to test public services or systems outside written programme scope.
